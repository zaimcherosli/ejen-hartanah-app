WHO WE ARE

Unrivaled Expertise in Malaysian Industrial & Commercial Property

Established as a dominant force in the commercial and industrial real estate sector, Corporate Estate Malaysia (CEM) has facilitated over RM 500 Million+ in cumulative transaction volume across key economic corridors in Selangor, Kuala Lumpur, Johor, Penang, and Negeri Sembilan.

We specialize in high-spec logistics warehouses, detached manufacturing facilities, zoned industrial land plots, and commercial retail headquarters. Our team combines deep technical due diligence (power supply amps, ceiling clearances, floor load capacity) with comprehensive regulatory advisory for Department of Environment (DOE), MIDA, and local council operating permits.
